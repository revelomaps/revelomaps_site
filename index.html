About Revelo Maps

Meet the Founder

Sheshu O'Hara

Hi, I'm Sheshu O'Hara, founder of Revelo Maps.

I started Revelo Maps because I noticed something surprisingly common: many therapy practices had Google Business Profiles that weren't being maintained. Reviews went unanswered, updates became inconsistent, and profiles no longer reflected the professionalism of the practice itself.

I believe therapists should spend their time helping people—not worrying about writing Google Posts, responding to reviews, or keeping their business profile up to date.

That's why I built Revelo Maps.

My goal is simple: to make Google Business Profile management easy, consistent, and reliable for therapists and counselors. I use AI to streamline the work, but every post, review reply, and recommendation is carefully reviewed before it's delivered.

Revelo Maps is a founder-led business built on quality, consistency, and honest communication. I work closely with every client to ensure their Google Business Profile stays active, professional, and accurately represents their practice.

As Revelo Maps grows, I'll continue expanding the business while keeping the same commitment to personalized service and high-quality work.

Thank you for visiting Revelo Maps. I look forward to helping you take one more task off your plate so you can focus on supporting your clients.
